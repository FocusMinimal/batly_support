Batly Support
Batly lives in your Mac's menu bar and wakes up when your battery gets low: at three levels you choose, a little pixel bat flies onto your screen so you can't miss it.

Need help? Write to support@selmanustun.com. I read every message.

Frequently asked questions
Where is Batly?
Batly has no Dock icon. Look for the small bat in the menu bar at the top of your screen and click it to see your battery and open Settings….

How do I change when Batly warns me?
Open Settings… › Alerts and drag the three pins on the battery. Each level (Gentle, Important, Urgent) can also have its own sound, be switched off, or be tried with Try the alert.

How do I make Batly go away?
Click Batly or its message box. Plugging in your charger also sends it away.

Can I keep Batly quiet for a while?
Yes: turn on Quiet for 1 hour in the menu. Batly waits quietly in the corner and only the most urgent level plays its sound. When the hour is over, any alert that is still relevant shows up.

Batly doesn't fly, it just hangs in the corner.
Reduce motion is on in System Settings › Accessibility › Display. Batly respects it and stays still.

Batly doesn't start when I turn on my Mac.
Turn on Open Batly when the Mac starts in Settings… › General, or in System Settings › General › Login Items.

Can I change Batly's language?
Yes: Settings… › General › Language. Batly speaks 12 languages and restarts to switch.

I'd like to see the welcome tour again.
Settings… › About › Show the tour again.

Does Batly work on a Mac without a battery?
Batly needs a built-in battery (MacBook). On a desktop Mac it shows "No battery found on this Mac" and gives no alerts.

Does Batly collect my data?
No. Batly cannot connect to the internet and collects no data. See the privacy policy.

How do I uninstall Batly?
Choose Quit Batly in the menu, then drag Batly from the Applications folder to the Trash.

Requirements: macOS 14 Sonoma or later, Apple silicon or Intel.
